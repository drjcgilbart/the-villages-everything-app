| `evening-jazz.mp3` | Evening Jazz Cart Ride |

| `golden-hour.mp3` | Golden Hour Portfolio |
| `cart-parade.mp3` | Parade of Golf Carts |

Sources: free stock instrumental libraries (Pixabay audio CDN downloads).

To swap in your own free tracks later, replace the MP3 files (keep the same filenames) or update paths in `src/lib/themeMusic.ts`.
