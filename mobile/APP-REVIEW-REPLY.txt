2) PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
The Villages Everything App is a free community companion for residents and neighbors of The Villages, Florida (an active-adult community). It solves the problem of scattered local information by putting village directories, dining ratings, rec centers, town squares, calendar, golf/pickleball tools, moderated yard sale, and a private “My Space” dashboard in one place.

It is not affiliated with The Villages® operators or developers. Built by a resident (Jonathan Gilbart) for neighbors. Target audience: adults living in or visiting The Villages, Florida. Not directed at children.

Public website (same content the iOS app displays): https://www.thevillageseverythingapp.com
Privacy: https://www.thevillageseverythingapp.com/privacy
Safety (report / block / delete account): https://www.thevillageseverythingapp.com/safety
Support: jonathan@thevillageseverythingapp.com

3) HOW TO ACCESS MAIN FEATURES
The iOS app is a native shell (splash, icon, safe area) that opens the live website.

No site-wide beta password is required.

Public features (no account): Home, Dining, Rec Centers, Town Squares, My Village directory, Calendar, Golf Zone, Pickleball, Local Pros, Real Estate, Forums (read).

Optional free membership (Porch Waver):
- In the app tap My Space or Request membership
- Register with name, email, password (8+ characters)
- Pending members can sign in; posting yard-sale listings waits for admin approval
- Reviewer demo account is already approved (no purchase needed)

Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1v):
Sign in → My Space → Membership tiers tab → scroll to “Delete my account” → type DELETE → Permanently delete account.
That removes membership, private My Space boards, and the session. Public posts remain as “Deleted neighbor.” Yard-sale listings are taken down.

User-generated content:
- Community Forums (posts/replies)
- Dining reviews
- Community Yard Sale listings (admin-approved before they go live)
- Best of the Month and Local Pros submissions (admin-approved)

Report and Block:
- Report is on forum posts, yard-sale listings, and dining reviews (no sign-in required to report)
- Block requires sign-in; blocked neighbors’ posts stay hidden for that member
- Reports email the host and appear in Admin → Safety; we hide/remove violating content, usually within 24 hours

Paid Hub membership ($1 / $2 / $3 per month) and optional tips are sold only on the website in Safari, not inside the iOS app. The store app does not offer In-App Purchase. Reviewers do not need to pay. All public directories and the free Porch Waver account work without payment.
